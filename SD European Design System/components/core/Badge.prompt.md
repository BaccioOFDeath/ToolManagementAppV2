One-line: uppercase status pill for stock, warranty, condition and promo flags.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>In Stock</Badge>
<Badge tone="accent">12 Month Warranty</Badge>
```

Tones map to meaning, not decoration: success = in stock / guaranteed fit, warning = low stock or back-order, danger = discontinued, info = special order, accent = promo, dark = "Used", neutral = everything else.

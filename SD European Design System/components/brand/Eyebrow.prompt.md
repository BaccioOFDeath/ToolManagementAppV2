One-line: the uppercase, 0.22em-tracked micro-label that precedes almost every SD European section heading, and the format used for marque lists.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Parts &amp; Servicing</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="accent">AUDI | BMW | MERCEDES | MINI | SKODA | VW</Eyebrow>
```

Tones: `muted` (default, grey on light), `accent` (yellow, on black), `inverse` (translucent white, on photography).
